Sader still has no closure date: eleven years of broken promises

WhatsApp Telegram Facebook Download the card

On 27 November the Federation of Residents' Associations of Bilbao issued a press release, in Basque and Spanish, on the announced closure of Profersa.

A promise from 2015. In 2015 Bilbao City Council announced that Sader would leave its current site in the second half of 2018. That agreement was never honoured. With that precedent, the Federation says, the announced closure of Profersa, the fertiliser factory, does not resolve the main concern: Sader, the hazardous waste treatment plant, still has no closure date in Bilbao.

The data. The release recalls the CSIC study: lead deposition is four times the highest level recorded in the reference cities in Spain in San Ignacio, three times in Ribera de Deusto - Zorrotzaurre and twice in Zorroza. It adds that soil samples taken next to the plant, in Zorroza, showed lead at six times the legal limit for a park.

What comes next. The Federation will keep testing the soil, air and water in the area and will submit whatever evidence it can, for three reasons: the health of residents and their children cannot wait, the previous agreement was not honoured, and the one announced this week does not include Sader.

And it calls on families to march together on 1 December, at 6.30 pm, from Gran Vía 85, to defend the right to breathe clean air.
