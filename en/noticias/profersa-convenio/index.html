Sader stays. Profersa will close while making losses, and with compensation in the millions

WhatsApp Telegram Facebook Download the card

What has been agreed. On 25 November the municipal Governing Board gave initial approval to a planning agreement with the Agaleus group to bring forward the closure of Profersa, the fertiliser factory. After 20 working days of public consultation and the signing, it will have at most six months to stop production: around mid-2027.

What it costs. A valuation commissioned by the City Council puts the cost of the closure at just over 3 million euros. The Council will pay close to 60%, around 1.8 million, in proportion to the land it owns in the area; the rest is charged to the land readjustment project as a development cost. The land and the buildings will be valued separately.

Profersa arrives making losses. In June 2026 Agaleus, its sole shareholder, reduced the factory's share capital from 782,640 to 60,000 euros to offset losses, according to the notice published in the Official Gazette of the Companies Registry. And it had left the factory out of the move to the port: according to the agreement, the company told the Council that it could not relocate it to another site.

Sader stays. The hazardous waste plant is not part of the agreement. It remains in Zorroza, with a permit to move to the port that expires in June 2031 and with no published decision obliging it to close. In 2015 the Council already signed a 2.7 million agreement with Sader for it to leave in 2018.

The question. The agreement will be open to public consultation for 20 working days. One question remains unanswered: how were 3 million euros in compensation calculated for a loss-making factory that the group had already decided not to relocate? The demonstration on 1 December goes ahead.

Sources (in Spanish): El Correo, 26/11/2026; El Salto, 26/11/2026; Radio Popular, 26/11/2026; Official Gazette of the Companies Registry (BORME), 20/07/2026.
