More than 4,500 signatures call for Sader and Profersa to leave Bilbao

WhatsApp Telegram Facebook Download the card

The Sader-Profersa Kanpora platform is keeping open on Change.org the petition “Sader-Profersa Kanpora / Respira Bilbao”, addressed to the Basque Government. As of 27 November 2026 it has 4,572 verified signatures, 237 of them in the last week.

It asks the Basque Government not to renew the environmental permit of Sader and Profersa at their current site, to work with Bilbao City Council and the Agaleus group to find a suitable and safe location away from the built-up area, and for the relocation to respect the workers' rights.

In its latest update, dated 26 November, the platform points out that the agreement announced concerns Profersa and that Sader still has no date for leaving Bilbao, and calls people to the demonstration on 1 December.
